# Joris Bijles Almere

Create a modern, professional personal tutoring website for Joris Abma, a private tutor based in Almere, Netherlands.

The website should be in Dutch and target parents and secondary-school students looking for tutoring.

Joris has a BSc in Business Administration with a major in Economics from Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, completed in 2026 with a GPA of 7.0/10. He has been tutoring since 2025. Students he has supported have improved from approximately grades 4–5 to 7–8.

Subjects: mathematics, physics, chemistry and economics.

Offer both online tutoring throughout the Netherlands and in-person tutoring in Almere and surrounding areas.

Availability: Monday–Thursday and Saturday all day. Wednesday only until 18:00.

Create a premium but approachable design. Clean typography, lots of whitespace, subtle blue/navy accents, professional photography, and a strong mobile design.

Include these sections: Hero, About Joris, Subjects, How lessons work, Experience/results, Availability, Pricing, FAQ, Reviews, and Contact.

The main call-to-action should be “Plan een gratis kennismaking”. Include a WhatsApp contact button and a contact form.

Make the website SEO-friendly for searches such as “bijles Almere”, “wiskunde bijles Almere”, “natuurkunde bijles Almere” and “online bijles”.

Do not make it look like a generic AI-generated template. Make it look like a real premium tutoring business.
